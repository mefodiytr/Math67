## English with the Bell Fairy 🔔 — Final Cambridge

**Final 4-step Reasoning Task.**

> *(a) The line y = kx + b passes through (0, 5) and (3, 14). Find k and b.*
> *(b) Solve the system: { y = 2x + 1; y = −x + 7 }.*
> *(c) Tariff A: £50 + £6/hour. Tariff B: £80 + £3/hour. After how many hours are they equal?*
> *(d) Lena's distance-time graph: she walks at 4 km/h for 2 h, rests for 30 min, then runs back at 8 km/h. Sketch the graph and find total time.*

> [Перевод для учителя.]
> «(a)/(b)/(c)/(d) реши.»

> **Методическая заметка.**
> (a) b = 5 (из (0, 5)); 14 = 3k + 5 → k = 3.
> (b) 2x + 1 = −x + 7 → 3x = 6 → x = 2; y = 5.
> (c) 50 + 6h = 80 + 3h → 3h = 30 → h = 10.
> (d) Расст 8 км. Возвращение: 8/8 = 1 ч. Всего: 2 + 0.5 + 1 = **3.5 ч**.
