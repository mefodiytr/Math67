## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour glow. Palette: imperial gold #F59E0B, charcoal #0F172A, faint teal highlights.

A small magical artifact: a perfectly polished sphere of warm amber-gold colour, with a single elegant sumi-e brushstroke forming the letter **x** (italic script) etched on its surface. The letter glows with a soft inner teal light. Smooth reflective surface. Centred composition, hovering slightly, lit from above. Faint golden aura.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.

## Заметки

- Сфера — символ «бесконечности значений переменной».
- **x** — стилизованная, не печатная буква (рукописная sumi-e).
- Сочетание золота и бирюзы — фирменное сочетание модуля 3.
