## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour glow. Palette: imperial gold #F59E0B, forest green #10B981, milky highlights.

A small magical artifact: a polished tetrahedron (four equilateral triangle faces), translucent gold with forest-green inner glow. Subtle Pythagorean triangle pattern visible on one face. Sharp clean edges. Centred composition, hovering slightly, slight rotation showing three faces. Soft golden aura.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.
