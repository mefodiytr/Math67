## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour glow. Palette: violet purple #7C3AED, deep indigo #1E3A8A, amber #FBBF24 lantern-glow.

A small magical artifact: a stone shaped like a fork in a path — a small Y-shaped stone with three smooth rounded ends. Translucent violet-purple with subtle inner amber glow, as if a tiny lantern lives inside. Sumi-e ink swirls on the surface suggest branching paths. Centred composition, hovering slightly, three-quarters view. Soft violet-amber aura.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.

## Заметки

- **Y-форма / развилка** — главная идея «выбор путей».
- Фиолетовый — необычный цвет, выделяется среди 9 камней.
- Внутри светится янтарный «фонарик».
