# feather-of-adept — Перо Адепта

**Категория:** artifacts
**Из модуля:** 10 (Лес Историй)
**Размер:** 1024×1024
**Фон:** прозрачный

## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour glow. Palette: milky white #F8FAFC, silver #E2E8F0, deep indigo #1E3A8A subtle highlights.

A single elegant white feather, long and slender, with delicate barbs catching soft moonlight. Along the central shaft, **nine** evenly-spaced tiny silver crescent-moon marks glow softly — one per Moon Stone collected. The feather floats vertically, slightly tilted, three-quarters view. Soft silver-indigo aura. The tip of the feather is faintly luminous as if dipped in starlight ink.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.

## Заметки

- **9 полумесяцев** на стержне — отсылка к 9 Лунным Камням.
- Перо — белое, лёгкое, не «ангельское» (без пышности).
- Идея: ученица теперь может «писать историю» сама.
