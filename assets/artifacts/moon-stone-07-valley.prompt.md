## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour glow. Palette: forest green #10B981, teal #0EA5E9, earthy brown highlights.

A small magical artifact: a polished cube with subtly cracked surfaces, translucent forest green with teal inner glow. Each face shows a faint sumi-e brushstroke suggesting measurement marks (like a ruler). The cracks are not damage — they look like measurement lines etched in. Centred composition, hovering slightly, slight rotation showing three faces. Soft green-teal aura.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.
