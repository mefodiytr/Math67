## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour glow. Palette: teal #0EA5E9, deep indigo #1E3A8A, silver highlights.

A small magical artifact: a perfectly cut octahedron crystal (eight triangular faces), translucent teal and deep indigo, with a softly glowing inner core. Tiny silver pinpoints of light float inside, suggesting prime numbers as scattered stars. Sharp clean edges, refractive surfaces. Centred composition, hovering slightly, slight rotation showing three faces. Soft cyan aura around the crystal.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.
