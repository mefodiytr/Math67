## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour glow. Palette: silver #E2E8F0, deep indigo #1E3A8A, lotus pink #F472B6 hint.

A small magical artifact: a polished lotus-shaped stone weight (like a small bell-shaped pendulum) in silver with subtle indigo veining. Five symmetrical lotus-petal segments rise from a circular base, joining at a small point on top. A soft silver-lunar aura around it, suggesting perfect balance. Centred composition, hovering slightly, three-quarters view. The stone seems to be perfectly poised.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.
