# crystal-of-axioms — Кристалл Аксиом

**Категория:** artifacts
**Из модуля:** 11 (Зал Девяти Зеркал)
**Размер:** 1024×1024

## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour glow. Palette: pure clear / silver #E2E8F0, faint refractive rainbow highlights, deep indigo background reflections.

A perfectly cut hexagonal prism crystal — six rectangular faces with two hexagonal ends — pure transparent quartz, refracting soft light. Inside the crystal, faintly visible thin sumi-e brushstrokes form abstract logical symbols (∀, ⇒, ¬) — but blurred, almost dreamlike. Centred composition, hovering slightly, three-quarters view, prism on its side. A subtle prismatic light scatter around the crystal.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.
