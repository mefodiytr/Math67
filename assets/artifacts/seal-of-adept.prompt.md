## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth meets Sailor Moon brightness, with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour glow. Palette: crimson #7F1D1D, scarlet #E11D48 highlights, imperial gold #F59E0B, silver #E2E8F0, deep indigo #1E3A8A core.

The grand final emblem: a softly glowing silver circular medallion. Inside the silver ring, **nine** evenly-spaced crescent-moon engravings all glow brightly in different module colours (lotus pink, teal, gold, silver, scarlet, gold, green, violet, indigo). Above the moons — a small white feather mark. To the side — a tiny six-faceted crystal mark. **Around the entire silver ring**, a glowing crimson ring (the Seal proper) frames the whole emblem with a soft fiery aura. Centred composition, front view, slightly tilted. The whole artifact glows with an imperial-gold and crimson aura, suggesting completion and mastery.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.
