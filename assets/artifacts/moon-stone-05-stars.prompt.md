# moon-stone-05-stars — Лунный Камень Звёзд

**Категория:** artifacts
**Из модуля:** 5 (Звёздная Карта)
**Размер:** 1024×1024
**Фон:** прозрачный

## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth meets Sailor Moon brightness, with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour glow. Palette: scarlet #E11D48, deep indigo #1E3A8A, golden #F59E0B sparkle.

A small magical artifact: an eight-pointed star (octogram) gem, faceted like a precious stone, glowing with a deep scarlet inner light. Subtle golden halo. Each of the 8 points slightly tapered. Centred composition, hovering slightly, front view. Tiny golden star-sparkles drift around it.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.
