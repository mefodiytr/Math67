## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli meets Sailor Moon brightness, with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures. Palette: scarlet #E11D48, imperial gold #F59E0B, milky white #F8FAFC.

A small fairy hovering in the air, palm-sized. Wearing a scarlet-and-gold kimono with golden trim and a wide red obi-belt. Black hair tied up in a high ponytail with a golden ornament. Holding a tiny silver bell on a red cord, mid-ring. Translucent fiery-red wings catching warm light. Three-quarters view, confident bright smile. A faint scattering of red sparkles around her.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.
