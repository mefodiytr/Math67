# kitsune — Кицунэ, Девятихвостая Лиса-Сказительница

**Категория:** characters
**Используется в:** модули 10, 12
**Размер:** 1024×1024
**Фон:** прозрачный

## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth and Japanese folklore (kitsune spirit), with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures. Palette: milky white #F8FAFC, golden ear-tips, scarlet #E11D48 small accents, faint golden aura.

An elegant snow-white nine-tailed fox spirit (kitsune), sitting in a regal posture with all nine fluffy tails fanned out behind her in a graceful arc, each tail tipped with a small golden flame-glow. Slim build, refined features, large amber eyes — wise and slightly mischievous. Tall pointed ears with subtle golden inner-fur. A small red ribbon tied at her neck. Three-quarters view, calm composed expression. A faint shimmer of paper-scroll motifs hovers in the background atmosphere.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.

## Заметки

- **9 хвостов** — обязательно (миф о девятихвостой лисе).
- Не «опасная демоническая» — мудрая сказительница.
- Свечение на кончиках хвостов — как огоньки у миифической девятихвостой.
