## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth (think Pom Poko / Heisei Tanuki) with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures. Palette: warm browns #92400E, milky white #F8FAFC, charcoal accents, gold sparkle.

A round-bellied tanuki (Japanese raccoon dog) standing on his hind legs, mid-mischief grin. Brown-and-cream fur, dark facial mask, fluffy striped tail. Wearing a small straw conical hat tilted to one side and holding a folded paper fan in one paw. A tiny coin purse at his belt. Cheerful trickster expression. Front three-quarters view.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.

## Заметки

- **Не енот** (raccoon) — японский тануки (raccoon dog).
- Толстый и круглый — как фигурки в саду.
- Мотив обмана: фан, шляпа, кошелёк — но добродушный, не злой.
