# lena — Лена (Ленcая), главная героиня

**Категория:** characters
**Используется в:** все модули (0..12)
**Размер:** 1024×1024
**Фон:** прозрачный

## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth meets Sailor Moon brightness, with subtle East-Asian sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures, gentle cel-shading, dreamy atmospheric lighting. Palette: deep indigo #1E3A8A, silver #E2E8F0, teal #0EA5E9, scarlet #E11D48, lotus pink #F472B6, imperial gold #F59E0B, forest green #10B981, charcoal #0F172A on milky white #F8FAFC. Composition: clean, airy, with negative space.

A 12-year-old girl, slim, gentle face, dark chestnut hair in two shoulder-length braids tied with small silver ribbons. Bright curious hazel eyes. Wearing a navy school cape clasped with a silver star brooch over a white turtleneck and pleated grey skirt, knee socks, dark Mary-Jane shoes. On her right wrist — a softly glowing silver circular emblem (the Adept badge). Standing in three-quarters view, calm friendly expression, slight smile, hands at sides. Full body, centred composition.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos, no signatures.
