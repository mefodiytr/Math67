# kuro — Куро, Кот-Детектив

**Категория:** characters
**Используется в:** модули 8, 11, 12
**Размер:** 1024×1024
**Фон:** прозрачный

## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures. Palette: charcoal #0F172A, deep indigo #1E3A8A, imperial gold #F59E0B accents, milky white highlights.

A sleek black short-haired cat with a tiny dark detective hat (deerstalker style) perched on his head. Standing on his hind legs in a confident pose, front paws gesturing as if explaining something. Round wide golden-yellow eyes — sharp and observant. Long flexible tail curving behind. A small magnifying glass tucked under one arm. Front three-quarters view, slight clever smirk.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.

## Заметки

- Шляпка-deerstalker — обязательный признак (Шерлок-стиль).
- Лупа — опционально.
