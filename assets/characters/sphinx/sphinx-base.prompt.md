**Категория:** characters

## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth with sumi-e ink accents and ancient-Egyptian motifs. Watercolour textures. Palette: imperial gold #F59E0B, deep indigo #1E3A8A, sand-beige, charcoal accents.

A regal Egyptian sphinx-cat — feline body lying in the classic recumbent pose, paws extended forward. Sleek short golden-tan fur with subtle hieroglyph-like sumi-e patterns along the flanks. A small ornate Egyptian-style headpiece with a single golden disc between her ears (like Hathor's sun disc). Bright golden eyes, deep wise gaze. Symmetrical front-facing composition. A subtle golden glow behind her, hinting at riddles.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.
