## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth with sumi-e ink accents and Japanese folklore atmosphere. Watercolour textures. Palette: silver #E2E8F0, fog #94A3B8, deep indigo #1E3A8A, violet glow.

A slender silver-grey two-tailed cat (a bakeneko of Japanese folklore), sitting elegantly with both forked tails curving up behind her in a graceful arc. Long fur with a soft silvery sheen, a faint violet aura. Mysterious bright violet eyes — slightly slanted. A subtle moonlight halo behind her. Three-quarters view, calm enigmatic expression with the hint of a knowing smile.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.
