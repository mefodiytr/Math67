# baihu — Байху, Белый Тигр-Капитан

**Категория:** characters
**Используется в:** модуль 7
**Размер:** 1024×1024
**Фон:** прозрачный

## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures. Palette: milky white #F8FAFC, silver #E2E8F0, charcoal black stripes, deep indigo #1E3A8A background highlights, faint starlight gold.

A large majestic white tiger of the Chinese zodiac, sitting in a regal posture. Snow-white fur with charcoal stripes that subtly resemble constellations — tiny silver stars are scattered along the stripes as if his coat held the night sky. Bright pale-blue eyes, calm wise expression. Side-three-quarters view, head turned toward the viewer. Lightly glowing aura of starlight around his shoulders.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.

## Заметки

- «Звёзды в шерсти» — главный мифологический признак (Белый Тигр Запада в китайской астрологии).
- Не агрессивный — стратег, мудрый.
- Размер — гигантский тигр, не котёнок.
