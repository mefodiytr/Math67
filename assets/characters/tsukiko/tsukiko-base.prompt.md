# tsukiko — Цукико, Лунная Кошка

**Категория:** characters
**Используется в:** все модули (главный куратор)
**Размер:** 1024×1024
**Фон:** прозрачный

## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth meets Sailor Moon brightness, with subtle East-Asian sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures, gentle cel-shading. Palette: deep indigo #1E3A8A, silver #E2E8F0, teal #0EA5E9 on milky white #F8FAFC.

A long-haired snow-white cat with a slender elegant build, sitting in a regal posture, three-quarters view. A small softly glowing silver crescent moon mark in the centre of her forehead. Large gentle eyes — pale teal with silver glints. Long fluffy tail curled around her paws. A faint silver shimmer in her fur, as if dusted with starlight. Wise, calm expression — a touch of saracsm in the gaze.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.

## Заметки

- Полумесяц на лбу — главный опознавательный знак.
- При малом размере читается по белизне + полумесяцу.
- Архетип: Артемис (Sailor Moon) × Кики (Ghibli).
