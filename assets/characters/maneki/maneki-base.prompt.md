## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth meets Sailor Moon brightness, with subtle East-Asian sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures, gentle cel-shading. Palette: imperial gold #F59E0B, ginger orange, milky white #F8FAFC, charcoal #0F172A.

A plump round-bodied ginger maneki-neko (Japanese beckoning cat), sitting upright on his haunches, his right paw raised in the classic beckoning gesture. Round friendly face, slit cat-eyes warm and amused, ginger tabby fur with white belly and chest. A small red collar with a tiny golden bell. A faint golden glow around his raised paw, as if signalling welcome. Cheerful, playful expression.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.
