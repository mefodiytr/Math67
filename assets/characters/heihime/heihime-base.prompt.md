## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures. Palette: charcoal #0F172A, imperial gold #F59E0B, lotus pink #F472B6 accents, milky white highlights.

An elegant black panther in anthropomorphic posture — sitting upright like a noble figure, wearing a flowing dark kimono with a subtle pink lotus pattern along the hem. Two large golden hoop earrings. Glossy jet-black fur with subtle violet sheen, sharp golden eyes. Calm aristocratic expression, slight knowing smile. Front-three-quarters view, regal posture, paws folded neatly.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.

## Заметки

- Не «гламурная пантера в платье» — строгая, элегантная.
- Кимоно — тёмное, лотос только акцентом.
- Серьги-кольца — обязательны.
