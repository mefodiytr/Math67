# cinglong — Цинлун, Лазурный Дракон

**Категория:** characters
**Используется в:** модули 3, 4, 7, 8, 10, 12
**Размер:** 1024×1024
**Фон:** прозрачный

## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth (think Haku from Spirited Away) with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures. Palette: teal #0EA5E9, deep indigo #1E3A8A, forest green #10B981, pearl-white highlights.

A small slender East-Asian serpentine dragon, body about a meter long, undulating gracefully through the air as if swimming. Sea-wave teal scales with paler pearl underbelly. Two small antlers, golden whiskers trailing from the snout, golden mane along the spine. Tiny clawed feet. Wise gentle expression with bright golden eyes. Coiling around an invisible axis, three-quarters view, mid-flight.

Transparent background. No readable text, no logos.
