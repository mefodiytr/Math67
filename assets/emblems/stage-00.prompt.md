## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted close-up illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth, with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures. Palette: silver #E2E8F0, faint indigo #1E3A8A glow.

A silver circular medallion (the Adept's emblem) seen from the front. The silver ring is polished but **the inner area is empty** — no engravings yet activated. Around the inner perimeter, 9 evenly-spaced **faint** crescent-moon outlines hint at where future moons will appear, but they are barely visible — just etched silhouettes in matte silver. A subtle silver shimmer around the medallion. Centred composition, slight tilt for depth.

Transparent background. No readable text.
