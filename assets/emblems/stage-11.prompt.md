## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted close-up emblem, Ghibli-Sailor Moon style. Palette: full module spectrum + silver-white + clear crystal refractions.

A silver circular medallion. 9 crescent-moon engravings, **all glowing brightly** in their module colours. Above the medallion centre — a small **white feather mark** (silver-white). Just opposite, mirroring the feather — a small **hexagonal clear crystal mark** (six-faceted prism etched in silver, with a soft prismatic light scatter). Both extras glow softly. Centred composition. Rich multi-colour aura with prismatic light additions.

Transparent background. No readable text.
