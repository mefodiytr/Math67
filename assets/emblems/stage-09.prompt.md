# stage-09 — Эмблема: 9 Лун (после Модуля 9)

**Категория:** emblems
**После модуля:** 9 (Ночная Обсерватория)

## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted close-up emblem, Ghibli-Sailor Moon style. Palette: silver, lotus pink, teal, imperial gold ×2, scarlet, forest green, violet, deep indigo.

A silver circular medallion. 9 crescent-moon engravings, **all glowing brightly** in their respective module colours: 1-pink, 2-teal, 3-gold, 4-silver, 5-scarlet, 6-gold, 7-green, 8-violet, 9-indigo. The medallion is at full power — a magnificent multi-colour halo around it. Centred composition.

Transparent background. No readable text.
