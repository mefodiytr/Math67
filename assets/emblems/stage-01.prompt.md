**После модуля:** 1 (Сад Дробей)
**Размер:** 1024×1024

## Промт (final)

A soft hand-painted close-up emblem illustration in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth, with sumi-e ink accents. Palette: silver #E2E8F0, glowing lotus pink #F472B6.

A silver circular medallion. Around the inner perimeter — 9 evenly-spaced crescent-moon engravings. The **first** crescent (at the 12-o'clock position, slightly to the right — at position 1) glows softly with **lotus pink** light. The other 8 crescents are tarnished silver, barely visible. Soft pink-silver aura around the medallion. Centred composition, slight tilt.

Transparent background. No readable text.

## Заметки

- 1-й полумесяц = верхний (по часовой от 12-часа).
- Светится тёплым лотос-розовым.
