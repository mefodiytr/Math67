## Промт (final)

A grand ceremonial close-up emblem in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth meets Sailor Moon brightness, with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour glow, dramatic lighting. Palette: silver #E2E8F0, full spectrum of module colours (lotus pink, teal, imperial gold, silver, scarlet, gold, forest green, violet, deep indigo), milky white feather, prismatic crystal, deep crimson #7F1D1D, bright scarlet #E11D48 for the outer ring.

A silver circular medallion. 9 crescent-moon engravings, **all glowing brightly** in their module colours. A small white feather mark above the centre. A small hexagonal crystal mark opposite. **Around the entire silver medallion** — a glowing **crimson ring** (the Seal of the Adept), framing everything with a soft fiery aura that radiates outward. The whole emblem is at peak brightness — the moment of completion. Centred composition, slight glowing halo extending beyond the crimson ring.

Transparent background. No readable text.
