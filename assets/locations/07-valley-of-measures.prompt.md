# 07-valley-of-measures — Долина Измерений

**Категория:** locations
**Модуль:** 7
**Размер:** 1920×1080
**Фон:** сплошной

## Промт (final)

A wide cinematic landscape in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures. Palette: forest green #10B981, teal #0EA5E9, soft sky blue.

A vast green valley cradled between two gentle mountain ranges. Patches of cultivated rice fields divided into precise rectangular and triangular plots — like a giant living mosaic of measured shapes. A winding river traced with small wooden bridges. Tall poles with measurement marks stand here and there along the river bank. Soft summer light, gentle clouds. **No characters.**

No readable text, no logos (the measurement marks should be subtle stripes, not numbers).

## Заметки

- Поля как **геометрические фигуры** — главный мотив.
- Рисовая мозаика — органическая, не индустриальная.
