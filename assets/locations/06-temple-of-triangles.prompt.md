## Промт (final)

A wide cinematic landscape in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures. Palette: imperial gold #F59E0B, forest green #10B981, sand-beige.

A serene Shinto temple complex on a forested hilltop, with a striking architectural feature: triangular roof gables and pediments emphasised throughout. A traditional torii gate at the entrance, its top beam forming a clear triangle silhouette against the sky. Stone lanterns in triangular forms line a moss-covered path. Late afternoon golden light filters through tall pine trees. Subtle Pythagorean-triangle motifs etched into the temple stones. **No characters.**

No readable text, no logos.
