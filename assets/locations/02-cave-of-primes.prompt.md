# 02-cave-of-primes — Пещера Простых Чисел

**Категория:** locations
**Модуль:** 2
**Размер:** 1920×1080
**Фон:** сплошной

## Промт (final)

A wide cinematic landscape in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures. Palette: teal #0EA5E9, deep indigo #1E3A8A, silver glow.

The interior of a vast crystalline cave. Glowing teal-and-silver stalactites hang from the ceiling, each one shaped like a long crystal that subtly suggests a numerical form (clean geometric clusters). Stalagmites rise from the floor in matching pairs. A mirror-still underground pool reflects the glow. Air-bubbles of soft cyan light float through the cave. Misty atmospheric depth. **No characters.**

No readable text, no logos.

## Заметки

- Цвета — холодные кристальные.
- Сталактиты — намёк на числа без явных цифр.
