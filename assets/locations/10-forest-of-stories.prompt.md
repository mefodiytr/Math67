# 10-forest-of-stories — Лес Историй

**Категория:** locations
**Модуль:** 10
**Размер:** 1920×1080
**Фон:** сплошной

## Промт (final)

A wide cinematic landscape in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth (think Princess Mononoke forest) with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures. Palette: forest green #10B981, milky-white mist #F8FAFC, deep indigo shadows, faint scarlet #E11D48 highlights.

An ancient deep forest at twilight. Tall mossy trees with twisted trunks rise out of soft drifting mist. Leaves and bark have a faint glow as if each tree holds a story. Small softly luminous wisps float between trees. Soft scarlet shafts of late sun pierce the canopy. A barely visible winding path leads deeper into the forest. **No characters.**

No readable text, no logos.

## Заметки

- Атмосфера — **загадочная, но не страшная**.
- Деревья «дышат» историями.
- Лёгкая тревожность, чтобы было «куда углубляться».
