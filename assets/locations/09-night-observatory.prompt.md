## Промт (final)

A wide cinematic landscape in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth meets Sailor Moon brightness, with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures. Palette: deep indigo #1E3A8A, silver #E2E8F0, faint star-gold.

A tall slender stone tower with a copper-domed observatory at its peak, perched on a floating island under a deep indigo midnight sky. The dome is partially open, revealing a great brass telescope pointing at a luminous full moon. Star charts are scattered on a stone bench at the base of the tower. Stars in clear constellations spread across the sky. A soft silver moon-glow bathes the scene. **No characters.**

No readable text, no logos.
