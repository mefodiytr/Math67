## Промт (final)

A wide cinematic landscape in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth (think Spirited Away garden) with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures. Palette: lotus pink #F472B6, jasmine #FEF3C7, soft indigo background.

A tranquil East-Asian zen garden on a floating island. Pink cherry-blossom trees in full bloom along carefully raked white-pebble paths. Small wooden bridges over a winding stream. Floating petals drift gently. Stone basins along the path are subtly divided into segments — fractions of a whole. Late afternoon golden-pink light. Distant pagoda silhouette. Negative space sky. **No characters in this scene.**

No readable text, no logos.
