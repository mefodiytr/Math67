# 04-castle-of-balance — Замок Весов

**Категория:** locations
**Модуль:** 4
**Размер:** 1920×1080
**Фон:** сплошной

## Промт (final)

A wide cinematic landscape in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures. Palette: silver #E2E8F0, deep indigo #1E3A8A, gold accents.

A grand fortress hall with a vaulted East-Asian dragon-beam ceiling. In the centre — an enormous beautifully crafted balance scale: two large silver pans suspended from a central golden beam. Both pans are perfectly level, holding small stone weights. Soft moonlight streams through tall narrow windows. The hall floor is polished dark stone that mirrors the scale. Atmospheric haze. **No characters.**

No readable text, no logos.

## Заметки

- **Огромные весы в центре** — главный визуальный объект.
- Идеальное равновесие = метафора уравнения.
- Цвет — серебро и индиго (лунный замок).
