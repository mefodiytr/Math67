# 05-star-map — Звёздная Карта

**Категория:** locations
**Модуль:** 5
**Размер:** 1920×1080
**Фон:** сплошной

## Промт (final)

A wide cinematic landscape in the spirit of Sailor Moon brightness meets Studio Ghibli warmth, with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures. Palette: scarlet #E11D48, deep indigo #1E3A8A, golden #F59E0B sparkle.

A floating wooden pier extending out into a starry night sky over a deep cosmic sea. The pier is built like a Japanese temple bridge. Constellations of stars form a luminous coordinate grid above — tiny golden dots at intersection points like a cosmic graph paper. A few brighter scarlet-glow stars scattered across the grid, marking key points. Reflections shimmer on the dark water below. Soft cosmic mist. **No characters.**

No readable text, no logos.

## Заметки

- Координатная сетка из звёзд — тонкая, не «графический инфографик».
- Атмосфера — космическая, но тёплая.
