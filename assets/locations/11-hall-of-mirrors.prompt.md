# 11-hall-of-mirrors — Зал Девяти Зеркал

**Категория:** locations
**Модуль:** 11
**Размер:** 1920×1080
**Фон:** сплошной

## Промт (final)

A wide cinematic interior in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures. Palette: silver #E2E8F0, deep indigo #1E3A8A, faint imperial gold accents.

A vast circular marble hall with high vaulted ceiling. Around the perimeter — **nine** tall slender silver-framed mirrors, each set into a stone arch, evenly spaced. Each mirror glows faintly with cool silver light. A polished dark stone pedestal stands in the centre of the hall. Subtle sumi-e brushwork etched into the floor in concentric circles. Cool moonlit atmosphere coming through high windows. **No characters.**

No readable text, no logos.

## Заметки

- **9 зеркал** — точно девять, равномерно по окружности.
- Пьедестал в центре пуст (заполнится в финале модуля).
- Архитектура классическая, без избытка деталей.
