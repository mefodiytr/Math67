## Промт (final)

A wide cinematic landscape in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth meets Sailor Moon brightness, with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures. Palette: crimson #7F1D1D, scarlet #E11D48, imperial gold #F59E0B, deep indigo #1E3A8A shadows.

A long majestic stone bridge arching gracefully across a vast cosmic chasm between two floating islands. The bridge is built of warm-toned stone with carved railings showing subtle East-Asian motifs. Above the bridge, a giant **crimson full moon** dominates the sky, casting a deep red-gold glow over the entire scene. Eighteen evenly-spaced bridge stones along the path subtly glow with warm light. The bridge stretches into a hopeful golden horizon at the far end. Cool indigo cosmic mist swirls below. **No characters.**

No readable text, no logos.
