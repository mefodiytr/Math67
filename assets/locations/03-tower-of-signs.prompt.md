# 03-tower-of-signs — Башня Знаков

**Категория:** locations
**Модуль:** 3
**Размер:** 1920×1080
**Фон:** сплошной

## Промт (final)

A wide cinematic landscape in the spirit of Studio Ghibli warmth with sumi-e ink accents. Watercolour textures. Palette: imperial gold #F59E0B, charcoal #0F172A, milky highlights.

A tall multi-tiered East-Asian pagoda rising into a starlit sky on a floating island. Each tier glows softly with golden light. Floating around the pagoda — small softly luminous variable symbols (x, y, z, n) drifting like fireflies, each formed by a single sumi-e brushstroke. The pagoda has a small stone garden at its base. Twilight blue sky with first stars appearing. **No characters.**

No readable text on signs / banners (the floating x/y/z are decorative single-stroke symbols, not text).

## Заметки

- **Парящие переменные** — главный визуальный мотив.
- Архитектурно: классическая пагода 5–7 ярусов.
